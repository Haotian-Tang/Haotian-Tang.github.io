I use statistics, genomics, and machine learning to study human health. My research spans genetic variation in neurological disease, single-cell immune responses, and the prediction of adverse drug reactions.

I am a PhD student at MBZUAI, supervised by Eduardo Beltrame and Jun Wen. Previously, I earned an M.S. in Biostatistics at Columbia University and an Honours B.Sc. at the University of Toronto, with training in pharmacology, genome biology, and mathematics.
